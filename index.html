About

Research at the boundary between intelligence and the physical world.

I am an AI and robotics researcher at Changwon National University. My work focuses on learning-based decision-making for autonomous systems, especially problems where multiple agents must collaborate under uncertainty.

I am particularly interested in making AI useful beyond simulation: connecting perception, reasoning, control, and real robotic hardware so that intelligent agents can operate reliably in dynamic environments.
